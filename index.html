JYJ Auto Publisher

JYJ Auto Publisher is a personal publishing tool built and operated by a single creator (JYJella). It uploads the owner's own original videos, such as music, behind-the-scenes clips, vlogs and web novel promotions, to the owner's own TikTok account on a fixed schedule using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not publish content on behalf of any other TikTok user.
