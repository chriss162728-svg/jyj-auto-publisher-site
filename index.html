JYJ Auto Publisher

The publishing tool of JELLAS Entertainment for its official TikTok account @jyjella. It posts our own finished videos on a fixed schedule so that readers and listeners can find our work.

About JELLAS Entertainment

JELLAS Entertainment is an independent creative studio that makes original music, music videos and web novels. We own the rights to everything we publish. TikTok is one of the channels where we introduce our work to new audiences, together with our YouTube channel.

What we publish

Music and music videos

Original songs by our artist JYJella and duet music videos, promoting our album and releases.

Web novels

Short trailers for our original serialized web novels, to bring more readers and subscribers.

Behind-the-scenes and vlogs

Short clips that show how our songs and stories are made.

What this tool does

  • Signs in to our own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit (scope: user.info.basic).
  • Uploads our own finished videos to that account with TikTok's official Content Posting API (scopes: video.publish, video.upload).
  • Keeps a simple log of what was uploaded and when, so the same video is never posted twice.

The tool is used only for the @jyjella account. It is not offered to other users and never posts for any other TikTok user. We label our videos as AI-assisted content where TikTok requires it.

How we use the data

The access token and the upload log stay on our own computer. We do not collect data from visitors to this website, and we do not sell or share data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
