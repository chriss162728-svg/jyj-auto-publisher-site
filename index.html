JYJ Auto Publisher

JYJ Auto Publisher is a publishing tool operated by JELLAS Entertainment, the creator and rights holder of the TikTok account @jyjella. The account is a marketing channel for our commercial works: it promotes our original web novels to gain more readers and subscribers, and promotes our music album and releases (songs, music videos, behind-the-scenes clips and vlogs) to increase album sales.

The tool uploads our own finished videos to this single account on a daily schedule using TikTok's official Content Posting API, so that promotion is consistent. It is used only for this account, is not offered to other users, and does not publish content on behalf of any other TikTok user.
