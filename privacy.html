Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

JYJ Auto Publisher ("the Tool") is a private tool used only by JELLAS Entertainment to publish its own videos to its own TikTok account, @jyjella.

What the Tool accesses

With the account owner's authorization through TikTok Login, the Tool receives an access token for the @jyjella account and uses it only to upload our own videos and to read the status of those uploads.

What the Tool stores

The access token and a log of uploads (file name, time, and success or failure) are stored on our own computer. Nothing is stored about other TikTok users.

Sharing

The Tool does not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties, other than sending our videos to TikTok as requested by us.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, and the stored token and logs can be deleted at any time.

Children

The Tool is not directed at children and collects no data from visitors to this website.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the TikTok account @jyjella.
