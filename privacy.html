Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

JYJ Auto Publisher ("the Tool") is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

What the Tool accesses

With the owner's authorization through TikTok Login, the Tool receives an access token for the owner's TikTok account and uses it only to upload the owner's own videos and to read the status of those uploads.

What the Tool stores

The access token and a log of uploads (file name, time, and success or failure) are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored about other TikTok users.

Sharing

The Tool does not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties, other than sending the owner's videos to TikTok as requested by the owner.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the Tool's access at any time in the TikTok app settings and can delete the stored token and logs at any time.

Children

The Tool is not directed at children and collects no data from visitors to this website.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the TikTok account @jyjella.
